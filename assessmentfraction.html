Class 5 Math Assessment (2083/05/24)

Learning objectives

  1. Convert improper fractions into mixed numbers and mixed numbers into improper fractions.
  2. Add and subtract up to three fractions with like denominators accurately.
Questions (6 X 5 = 30)
  1. 1 . Sarita had gone to buy household goods in a wholesale shop. She bought 512 kg mungi and 2.5 kg musuro.
    1. Convert 2.5 into mixed fraction.
    2. If she has to make 1012 kg of dal by mixing mungi and mas, how many kgs of mas should she buy?
  2. 2 . The obtained marks of Mahesh of the second terminal exam is shaded in the figure alongside.
    1. Express the mark obtained by Mahesh in the fraction.
    2. Express the marks obtained by Mahesh in the decimal form.
    3. If mahesh's friend reshma obtained 15, who obtained more mark? Write with cause.
  3. 3 . On the basis of the concept of fraction, solve the following questions.
    1. Convert 263 into mixed fraction.
    2. Subtract: 456 - 216
    3. 314 + 524 + 734
  4. 4 . In the figure alongside, two complete and one forth of a circle are shaded. On the basic of observation of the given figure, solve the following questions.
    1. Write the fraction represented by shaded part on the above figure.
  5. 5 . From a bag of money, Ram took out 17, Hari took out 37, shyam took out 27, how much money have they taken out all together ?
  6. 6 . A Shopkeeper had 2012 kg potato. He sold 912 kg potato in the morning and 712 kg potato in the evening. How many potato is left with shopkeeper ?
