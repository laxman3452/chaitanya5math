🛒 Real-Life Supermarket

🥛 Problem 1

A family buys 3 bottles of milk. Each contains 2 L. How much milk?

2 × 3 = 6 L ✅

🧃 Problem 2

A juice shop has 5 L. It fills 1 L bottles. How many bottles?

5 ÷ 1 = 5 bottles ✅

💧 Problem 3

8 L is divided equally among 4 children. How much each?

8 ÷ 4 = 2 L per child ✅
